Ambit: what you, your agents, and your machines can jointly do

If you use AI agents, your setup is spread across LLM providers, MCP servers, local CLI tools, skills, credentials, and more than one machine. Ambit reads the configs of Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI and builds one capability graph out of them.

What it answers

  • What works right now, what is broken, and what is one dependency away.
  • What breaks downstream if a model, MCP server, tool or credential goes away.
  • What compound abilities emerge when two tools are combined.
  • What is worth setting up next, priced by the human attention it would save.

You ask from the terminal. Your agents ask over MCP: Ambit is itself an MCP server, with sixty tools and an approval step a person signs before any configuration changes. Everything stays in a local SQLite file.
